# Netflix Insights Repository

## Project Context
This repository analyzes Netflix's content catalog to understand content distribution patterns, temporal trends, and strategic focus areas. The analysis uses Netflix's public catalog data containing 8,807 titles spanning multiple decades.

## AI Watch Insights

### 1. Artificial Intelligence

#### 1.1 Introduction and Challenges

Artificial Intelligence represents a major technological revolution that Netflix leverages extensively:

* **Computer systems** designed to simulate human intelligence through complex algorithms and mathematical models
* **Performs complex tasks reserved for human intelligence** such as problem-solving, learning, pattern recognition, and autonomous decision-making
* **Numerous subfields** leveraged by Netflix include:
  * Machine learning
  * Deep learning
  * Natural Language Processing (NLP)
  * Computer vision
* **Promising applications at Netflix:**
  * **Content Personalization**: Advanced recommendation systems using viewing history and preferences
  * **Quality Optimization**: AI-driven streaming quality adjustment based on network conditions
  * **Content Creation**: Predictive analytics for content success probability
  * **User Experience**: Dynamic thumbnail selection and interface customization
  * **Accessibility**: AI-powered subtitle generation and audio descriptions
* **Ethical and Social Challenges:**
  * Responsible development of technologies to avoid harmful content recommendations
  * Avoiding biases in data that can perpetuate existing inequalities in content representation
  * Ensuring privacy and data protection in AI-driven recommendation systems

#### 1.2 AI in Service of Cultural Content

Netflix utilizes AI technologies to enhance how users discover and experience cultural content:

* **Content Discovery:**
  * **Deep Learning:** Automatic recognition and classification of visual content themes and patterns
  * **NLP:** Understanding complex language patterns to better match content with user preferences
  * **Predictive Analysis:** Planning content acquisition based on projected viewer engagement
* **Content Enhancement:**
  * **Intelligent Content Tagging** enabling more precise content discovery across cultural boundaries
  * **Customized viewing experiences** through AI-driven interface personalization
  * **Cross-cultural content recommendations** breaking traditional viewing pattern boundaries

### 2. Technology Focus on Three Specific Elements of AI at Netflix

#### 2.1 Machine Learning

Machine Learning forms the backbone of Netflix's recommendation system:

* **Core component of Netflix's technology stack** driving personalization at scale
* **Trains models to make predictions** about user preferences and content performance
* **Makes decisions** on content recommendations that adapt to user behavior over time
* **THREE MAJOR CATEGORIES OF METHODS** used by Netflix:
  * **Supervised learning**
    * **Labeled data** from user interactions and explicit ratings
    * Applications include personalized recommendations and viewer retention analysis
  * **Unsupervised learning**
    * **Unlabeled data** analysis to discover hidden content affinities
    * **Structure discovery** for identifying unexpected content relationships
  * **Reinforcement learning**
    * **Trial/error reward system** optimizing recommendation strategies based on user engagement

#### 2.2 Data Preprocessing

Data preprocessing is critical for Netflix's AI implementation:

* **Generates reliable data** for content analysis and user behavior modeling
* **Aligns data with analysis needs** specific to content recommendation and consumption patterns
* **Reduces biases and increases the efficiency of analyses** across global user segments
* **THREE STEPS** in Netflix's data pipeline:
  * **Data Cleaning**
    * **Missing Values, Duplicates** handling in viewing histories and content metadata
    * Normalization of viewing patterns across different devices and platforms
  * **Data Normalization**
    * **Scaling, Encoding** of user behavior metrics across different cultural contexts
    * Standardization of content metadata across international catalogs
  * **Data Transformation**
    * **Dimensionality Reduction** to simplify recommendation models while maintaining accuracy
    * Feature engineering to better capture viewing intent and satisfaction

#### 2.3 Descriptive Data Analysis

Descriptive analysis provides the foundation for Netflix's content strategy:

* **Foundation of Netflix's Data Science** approach to content acquisition and production
* **Main techniques:**
  * **Measures of central tendency** in viewing patterns across demographics
  * **Dispersion evaluation** of content performance across markets
  * **Visualization of distributions** to identify content gaps and opportunities
* **Benefits for Netflix's Data and AI:**
  * **Detects biases and improves data quality** in recommendation systems
  * **Facilitates interpretation and communication of insights** to content creation teams
  * **Guides algorithm selection and optimizes preprocessing** for different types of content
  * **Reduces modeling errors and overfitting** in recommendation engines

## Proposed Solutions

### Recommendations

#### 1. Series: The Strategic Challenge of Readjustment

**Observation: Double Issue**

1. **Catalog Imbalance:**
   - Currently, our catalog consists of 70% movies versus 30% series. However, series are three times more profitable in terms of subscriber retention. This imbalance needs to be addressed to maximize user satisfaction and loyalty.

2. **Premature Death of Series:**
   - 75% of our series are canceled before reaching Season 3, leading to a waste of creative talent and significant frustration among our subscribers. It is crucial to allow series to develop their audience over the long term.

**Our Solution: The "Double Rebalancing" (50/50)**

- **Convert 3 movie franchises into series per year:**
  - For example, transforming cinematic successes like *Extraction* into prequel series to capitalize on their popularity and expand their narrative universe.

- **Launch 10 mini-series (6 episodes) per year:**
  - These mini-series will replace underperforming movies, providing fresh and attractive content that can better retain subscriber attention.

- **Guarantee 2 seasons for every new series:**
  - Each new series will be guaranteed two seasons to allow it to build its audience. The third season will be conditional, based on performance and viewer engagement.

#### 2. Eliminate "Zombie Content"

**Alarming Observation**

- **Viewing Imbalance:**
  - Although 70% of our catalog consists of movies, they account for only 30% of the total viewing time. Additionally, thousands of works from before 2010 are never watched, representing an unnecessary cost.

**Our Solution: Ruthlessness**

- **Delist 20% of movies:**
  - We will remove movies from the catalog that generate fewer than 100 views per month and have a high licensing cost relative to their profitability.

- **Replace with:**
  - Add 1,000 hours of short Asian series, a highly demanded content by our subscribers.
  - Establish partnerships to include remastered cult films, thus adding value to our catalog.

#### 3. Make Asia the New Production Hub

**Opportunity**

- **Asian Potential:**
  - India, South Korea, and Japan account for 40% of the top content-producing countries but receive only 15% of Netflix's total budget. It is time to capitalize on this underutilized potential.

**Our Solution: Triple Penetration of the Asian Market**

- **India:**
  - Invest in low-cost regional series that meet local expectations and can attract a wide audience.

- **Korea:**
  - Double our investments in psychological thrillers, a genre extremely popular internationally.

- **Japan:**
  - Dominate the adult animation market, a niche under-exploited by Disney and with strong growth potential.

By implementing these recommendations, we can not only improve subscriber satisfaction but also optimize our resources for maximum impact.
